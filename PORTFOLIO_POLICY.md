# Portfolio No-AI-Slop Policy

Canonical base:
`skills/no-ai-slop/SKILL.md`

This repository is a maintained portfolio derivative of the original
No AI Slop project by Peter Yang, licensed under MIT.

## Applies to

All substantive user-facing copy in portfolio projects:

- landing pages
- articles
- knowledge pages
- comparison copy
- category copy
- product introductions
- SaaS onboarding
- explanatory UI copy
- transactional emails
- AI-generated reports
- FAQs
- metadata where relevant

## Additional portfolio rules

### Source discipline

Do not:
- invent facts;
- invent statistics;
- invent prices;
- invent legal claims;
- use vague attribution such as "experts say";
- convert uncertain facts into assertions.

### Vertical terminology

Project-specific terminology and factual rules override stylistic rewriting.

Never rewrite:
- legal quotations;
- regulatory text;
- technical identifiers;
- source IDs;
- API/schema values;
- evidence quotations.

### Dutch copy

Dutch pages must read as naturally written Dutch.

Avoid:
- literal English sentence structure;
- generic SaaS language;
- "in het huidige snel veranderende landschap";
- "innovatieve oplossing";
- "stelt bedrijven in staat om naadloos";
- unnecessary English business terminology.

### Portability test

If a sentence could be pasted unchanged onto a completely different
site, product, SaaS or vertical, either remove it or make it specific.

## Merge gate

User-facing copy is not considered complete until it has been checked
against:

1. `skills/no-ai-slop/SKILL.md`
2. this portfolio policy;
3. project-specific content/source rules.
